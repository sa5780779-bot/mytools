Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it when you visit Free Online Tools.

Information you enter

The tools on this website (age, EMI, percentage calculators and the QR code generator) run inside your browser. The numbers, dates, links or text you enter are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

Log and usage information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. This is used only for security and to understand how the site is used.

Cookies and advertising

We may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense to show ads on this website. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.

Third-party libraries

Some tools, such as the QR code generator, load a script from a content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) to work.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
