About This QR Code Generator

This free tool turns any link or text into a QR code that you can scan with a phone camera or download as an image. No signup is needed.

How to use it

1. Type or paste your link or text. 2. Click "Generate QR Code". 3. Scan it with your phone to test it. 4. Click "Download QR Code" to save the image.

What is a QR code?

A QR code is a square pattern of black and white dots that stores information such as a website link, phone number or plain text. A phone camera reads it instantly.

Where can you use it?

Put it on business cards, shop menus, posters, flyers, invitations or social media profiles so people can open your link without typing it.
