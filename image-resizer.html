About This Free Image Resizer

This free online image resizer changes the width and height of your photo in pixels or by percentage and lets you save it as JPG, PNG or WEBP. Everything happens inside your browser. Your photo is not uploaded to any server.

How to use it

  1. Click the box or drag and drop your image into it.
  2. Choose a quick size (25%, 50%, 75%) or type a new width or height. With "Keep aspect ratio" on, the other value changes automatically.
  3. Pick the format and, for JPG or WEBP, the quality.
  4. Click "Resize & Download Image". The new file size is shown after it is created.

Where is it useful?

Use it to make photos smaller for email, WhatsApp, forms and job or visa applications that ask for a size limit, to prepare images for a website, or to convert between JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. The image is processed on your own device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Will resizing reduce the quality?
Making an image smaller usually looks fine. Making it larger than the original makes it look soft or blurry because no new detail can be created. JPG and WEBP also compress the image, so a lower quality setting gives a smaller file with some loss of detail. PNG keeps all detail but the file is often bigger.

How can I make the file size smaller (in KB)?
Reduce the width and height, save as JPG or WEBP, and lower the quality slider a little. Try a few settings until the size suits you.

Which format should I choose?
JPG is best for photos. PNG is best for logos, screenshots and images with transparent areas. WEBP gives small files and works in modern browsers and apps.

What happens to transparent areas when I save as JPG?
JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white. Choose PNG or WEBP to keep transparency.

Is there a size limit?
Very large images (above about 10,000 pixels on a side, or around 80 million pixels in total) are not supported because browsers and phones limit how big an image can be. Photos from phones and cameras work fine.

Can I resize many images at once?
Not yet. This tool resizes one image at a time.

Why does my image not open?
Some formats, such as HEIC from certain iPhones, cannot be opened by every browser. Convert the photo to JPG or PNG first, or take a screenshot of it, and try again.
