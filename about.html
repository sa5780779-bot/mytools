About Us

Free Online Tools is a simple website that offers fast and easy tools for everyday use, such as an age calculator, EMI calculator, percentage calculator and QR code generator.

Our goal is to make common calculations and tasks quick, clear and free for everyone. All our tools work directly in your browser, and you do not need to create an account.

We keep adding new tools regularly. If you have an idea for a tool or found a mistake, please contact us.
