About This Age Calculator

This free age calculator tells you your exact age in years, months and days. Just select your date of birth and the tool compares it with today's date.

How to use it

1. Choose your date of birth from the calendar. 2. Click the "Calculate Age" button. 3. Your age appears instantly below the button.

Why is it useful?

You can use it to check your age for job applications, school admissions, passport and visa forms, or any form that asks for your exact age on a particular day.

How is age calculated?

The calculator subtracts your birth date from today's date. If your birthday has not come yet this year, one year is reduced, and the remaining months and days are adjusted automatically.
