About This Voice Recorder

This free online voice recorder uses your browser's microphone to record audio. Recording, trimming, effects and downloads all happen on your device. Your audio is not uploaded to our servers.

How to use it

  1. Choose your options (noise suppression, auto-pause, live transcription) and click the red mic button.
  2. Speak. Use Pause and Resume if you need a break. The live waveform shows that the mic is working.
  3. Click the mic again to stop. Drag the Trim sliders to cut the start or end.
  4. Pick a voice effect and a format (WAV, MP3 or WebM), then click Download Audio.

Features

Live waveform, noise suppression, pause on silence, trim, three voice effects, WAV, MP3 and WebM export, live transcription with copy and download, simple key points from your transcript, and automatic local recovery if your browser closes by accident.

Privacy note

Auto-recovery keeps your latest recording in your own browser storage until you download or discard it. If you turn on live transcription, your browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome) processes the audio to create text. Key points are made on your device from the transcript text and are not created by an AI model.

Good to know

If the mic does not work, check that your browser has microphone permission for this site and that no other app is using it. In a private or incognito window, auto-recovery does not work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my recording uploaded to your server?
No. Recording, trimming, effects and downloads happen on your device. The only exception is the optional live transcription, where your browser may send the audio to its own speech service (for example Google in Chrome) to create the text.

What is the difference between WAV, MP3 and WebM?
WAV is uncompressed, gives the best quality and plays almost everywhere, but the file is large. MP3 is much smaller and works on nearly every phone and player. WebM is a compressed format that works well in browsers but some apps and older devices may not open it.

Does the voice effect change the downloaded file too?
Yes. The effect you choose is applied to what you hear and to the file you download. Higher pitch also makes the voice faster, and deeper pitch makes it slower.

Why is my microphone not working?
Click the lock icon next to the web address and allow the microphone for this site. Also close other apps that may be using the microphone, such as video call apps, and check that the correct microphone is selected in your device settings.

What does noise suppression do?
It turns on your browser's built-in filter that reduces steady background sounds like a fan. It helps, but it cannot remove all noise, so recording in a quiet place still gives the best result.

How does pause on silence work?
When you stop talking for about a second and a half, the recorder pauses by itself and continues when you speak again. If it pauses while you talk, lower the silence level. If it does not pause, raise it.

Can I recover a recording if my browser closed?
Yes, in most cases. Your latest recording is kept in your browser's local storage, and a Recover button appears the next time you open this page. This does not work in private or incognito windows, and the saved copy is deleted when you download or discard it.

Does it work on a phone?
Recording works in most modern phone browsers. Live transcription depends on the browser, and it works best in Chrome and Edge. Very long recordings can be slow to process on older phones.
