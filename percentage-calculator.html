About This Percentage Calculator

This free percentage calculator solves the three most common percentage problems in seconds: finding a percentage of a number, finding what percent one number is of another, and calculating percentage increase or decrease.

How to use it

Pick the calculator that matches your question, type the two numbers and click "Go". The answer appears right below.

Formulas

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y. X is what % of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100. Percentage change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100.

Examples

20% of 500 is 100. 50 is 25% of 200. If a price goes from 100 to 150, it is a 50% increase.

Where is it useful?

Use it for discounts and sale prices, marks and exam results, salary increases, profit and loss, and tax or commission calculations.
